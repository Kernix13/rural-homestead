## Where to Look (Beyond Zillow)

> also Google `Tax lien auction` & `reeposseessed land`

1. County Clerk & PVA Offices - Check the Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) websites for nearby counties (like Madison, Rockcastle, Estill, Garrard, Jackson, etc.).

They often have searchable maps and recent sales data.

You can also call or visit the County Clerk to ask about upcoming delinquent tax sales or estate sales.

2. Facebook Marketplace & Local Groups - Search for land in groups like:

- Land for Sale in Kentucky

County-specific buy/sell/trade groups - Post what you're looking for—some sellers prefer to go private and avoid realtor fees.

3. Auction Websites - Check `GovDeals`, `AuctionZip`, and `Sohn & Associates` for Kentucky auctions - `nothing`

- Look at local auctioneers’ websites in counties within your search area (especially ones that specialize in estate or real estate auctions).

4. Craigslist (Still Useful for Rural Land) - Use the "real estate - by owner" section - `nothing`

- Search using broader terms like “land” or “acreage” and try different nearby cities (not just Berea).

5. Talk to Local Farmers or Small-Town Realtors - Stop by or call small-town realty offices—they sometimes have pocket listings not posted online.

- Ask around at local feed stores, farmers markets, or even gas stations in rural areas.

6. Drive for Dollars - Take weekend drives through rural backroads and look for “For Sale By Owner” signs or vacant land with no signage.

- Keep a notebook or take photos, then look up the owner via county GIS or PVA tools.

## Ways to Find Land Not Listed on Zillow

1. Check Local Courthouse & County Clerk Offices - Why: Tax delinquent properties and estate sales often fly under the radar.

- Where: Ask about upcoming land auctions or tax sales in surrounding counties (like Madison, Rockcastle, Garrard, Jackson, and Estill).
- Bonus: These can sometimes be picked up for under market value.

2. Facebook Marketplace & Local Groups - Search: “Land for sale near Berea KY,” “Owner financing land KY,” etc. Join local groups like:

- Central KY Land and Property
- Kentucky Off-Grid & Homestead Market
- KY Land for Sale by Owner

3. Craigslist (Lexington, Richmond, London areas) - Look under “real estate - by owner” and try keyword variations: “acreage,” “raw land,” “homestead,” "owner finance."

4. Drive Around (Seriously) - In rural areas, landowners sometimes just put up signs: “Land for sale – Call [number].” Bring a GPS and camera, and jot down parcel info if posted.

5. Talk to Local Real Estate Agents Specializing in Land - Some agents have “pocket listings” not posted online. Ask if they know of any FSBO (for sale by owner) leads or upcoming parcels.

6. Check Auction Sites. Try:

- [AuctionZip](https://www.auctionzip.com/)
- [GovDeals](https://www.govdeals.com/)
- [HiBid](https://www.hibid.com/)
- Filter by state and county, and look for land/real estate categories.

7. Contact Local Surveyors or Title Companies - They often know about landowners who are preparing to sell or divide land. Friendly call or email goes a long way.

8. Look at Plat Maps / GIS - Most counties have an online GIS portal. You can:

- Identify vacant land parcels.
- See owner names and mailing addresses.
- Reach out directly with a letter of interest (old-school but works).
