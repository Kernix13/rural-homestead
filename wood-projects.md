1. How to Build an Ottoman

- https://youtu.be/1veslaw5ODg?si=A7Zf5CbWRl-zVnqA
- Cushion at a local home store
- Table saw to cut:
- Baltic Birch plywood

Rabbit joint

- using the table saw - changed to a different type of saw blade - "dado" blade?
- looks like a groove on the inside around all 4 sides on the 2 short sides or maybe all sides (not sure)
- draw lines on the sides a 1/4" in from each edge as a guide for nailing
  - _how deep was the rabbit joint made_?
- apply glue to the rabbit joint
- secure together with 1.25" finish nails (how many?)
- use quick release clamps if needed
- connect all 4 sides - then measure the inside dimensions for the bottom
- cut it out - dry fit together before glueing
- glue it then draw lines on the sides a 1/4" in from each edge as a guide for nailing - secure together with 1.25" finish nails (how many?)
- fill all the nail holes with wood filler
- draw across them with that one tool I have (may be in storage)
- table saw to cut the lid, the lid stop and the feet
- the lid stops are thin pieces, maybe 1x3's
- you want to attach them to the inside of the box but first draw a line inside 3/4" down to give enough room for the lid and the cushion board which sits below the top surface so the cushion sits flat right on the top
- attach the lid stops first to the front and back boards with glue and 3/4" finish nails then attach the side lid stops
- for the cusion he is sandwiching the bottom fabric between 2 boards and attach the 2 boards in each corner using T nuts and flat head screws
- on the lid make marks on each corner 1.5" in
- drill a 1/4" hole at each mark
- attach t he T nuts to the cusion board at those holes
- unzip the cushion and slip the cusion board inside
- lay the lid on top of the cushion and center it with the cushion board
- poke thru the cushion fabric at all 4 holes using an awl
- sand the whole box with 220 grit sand paper and round over all the edges using a 1/4" rounder "whippet?"
- paint the box and feet
- use double sided tape to stick the feet to the box then nail from inside
- attach the lid to the cushion
